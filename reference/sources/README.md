Source rows remain disabled until the required human legal/access/persistence review.

`jonkoping-utegym.v1.json` records the positive SRC-02 source-policy gate for
the selected official municipal layer. Its corresponding seeded source is
enabled; unselected municipal layers remain absent.

policy. Only factual extracted metadata for single-occurrence records may be
retained. Multi-occurrence records remain unsupported; bounded `DELTA_ONLY`
ingestion is enabled and absence never implies disappearance or cancellation.
