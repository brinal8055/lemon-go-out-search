- `något att dricka på ett ställe med trevlig stämning` is deliberately literal to preserve the EN/SV pair.

## Potentially ambiguous intended semantics

- `något kul i stan` uses ordinary Swedish city-centre language while the hard scope remains the full municipality.
- `Centralen` deliberately tests an ambiguous alias and does not assert a protected known item.
- Contradictory mixed-language adversarial semantic queries preserve their structured hard filters rather than implying one preferred interpretation.
